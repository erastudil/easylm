# Studio

The Stacks are the library. Studio is the walk: lessons, practice, projects, essays, exams. The app is the teacher. No gatekeepers. No account. Study record on this device — not a diploma.

AI coach is aimed at **14 and up**. Household Kid Safe still works for younger profiles (Socratic, local Hands only). Middle-school tracks later. Elementary is far.

## Law (Boss 2026-09-13)

- **Pass / fail. No grades.** Completion, not letters, not percentages on a report card.
- **As many tries as it takes.** Tries are not a mark against you.
- **Reward persistence**, not first-try speed. First-try because you already knew is not the lesson.
- **No due dates.** A planned schedule exists so you can see a pace. Life happens. On Studio load, if you are behind, the plan shifts. Guidance, not judgement.
- **Soft timers** on exams: the suggested sitting length. Not a cutoff. Time running out does not fail you.
- **Local badges.** Motivation on this machine.

## Walk

Course pack → units → lessons → items: reading, assignment, quiz, project, essay, exam.

Wave 1: `methods-inquiry-1`, `math-calc-1`, `physics-college-1`, `chemistry-2e-1`, `biology-2e-1`, `civics-us-1`.

Readings are stack textbook chapters. Doors are official hosts already in `LINK_INDEX.md`. OpenStax CC BY items are adapted with attribution. MIT OCW / PhET / LibreTexts are links, not vendored bodies.

## Grade

`grade.ts` compares authored keys. The model coaches. It does not hold the key.

Quiz / exam pass = every question right. Retry any question. Misses become Leitner cards.

Essays and projects: rubric + self-score + Document Studio. The model does not invent a letter.

## Record

XP on pass is the same whether it took one try or ten. Extra XP only for passing after failure. Badges include early riser, night scholar, exam persistence (three failed sittings), and overcoming (pass after that).
