## 3. Key Concepts & Hermeneutic Anchors

| Concept | Chinese | Core Philosophical Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Dao (道)** | 道 | The generative, undifferentiated source of all phenomena; the cosmic order |
| **De (德)** | 德 | The latent power, integrity, or inner virtue through which things realize their nature |
| **Wu Wei (無為)** | 無為 | Effortless, non-coercive, frictionless action attuned to natural dynamics |
| **Pu (樸)** | 樸 | The uncarved block; primal simplicity and pure unconditioned potential |
| **Ziran (自然)** | 自然 | Spontaneity, self-so-ness, that which happens naturally of its own accord |
| **Yin & Yang (陰陽)** | 陰陽 | Complementary polarities in continuous dynamic equilibrium |
