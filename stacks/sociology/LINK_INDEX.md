# Sociology & Cultural Anthropology — Primary Academic Portals & Open Doors

1. **American Sociological Association (ASA)** — Scholarly resources, peer-reviewed journals (American Sociological Review), and ethical research standards.
   https://www.asanet.org/

2. **American Anthropological Association (AAA)** — Open access portals for ethnography, linguistic anthropology, and archaeological research.
   https://www.americananthro.org/

3. **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Max Weber & Émile Durkheim** — Detailed structural analyses of classical sociological foundations and social action theory.
   https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/weber/

4. **Marxists Internet Archive** — Complete, open-access public editions of classical writings by Karl Marx, Friedrich Engels, and historical materialist scholarship.
   https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/

5. **OpenStax Introduction to Sociology 3e** — Peer-reviewed, open-access college textbook covering sociological paradigms, institutions, stratification, and methods.
   https://openstax.org/details/books/introduction-sociology-3e

6. **United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs (DESA)** — Demographic datasets, urban sociology reports, and global population projections.
   https://www.un.org/development/desa/en/

7. **The British Journal of Sociology (LSE)** — Foundational articles on social stratification, class reproduction, and modern globalization.
   https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/14684446

8. **International Sociological Association (ISA)** — Global research committees on urban studies, social movements, and institutional sociology.
   https://www.isa-sociology.org/

9. **Human Relations Area Files (eHRAF)** — Cross-cultural ethnographic databases for cultural anthropology and kinship studies.
   https://hraf.yale.edu/

10. **MIT OpenCourseWare Anthropology & Sociology** — Lecture courses on ethnographic theory, cultural symbols, and institutional structures.
    https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/anthropology/
