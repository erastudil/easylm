Combining all pigments subtracts all light, producing dark brown or black. // door https://cie.co.at/ // ref 8.1 additive vs subtractive color mixing

intaglio printing : Lines are incised into a copper or zinc plate using a burin or acid bite. Ink is pushed into the grooves, and the surface is wiped clean. Tremendous mechanical pressure from a roller press forces damp paper into the incisions, picking up the ink. // door https://smarthistory.org/ // ref 9.2 atmospheric aerial perspective

planographic printing : Based on the chemical antipathy of oil and water. The artist draws with greasy crayons on limestone, etches it with gum arabic and nitric acid, wets the stone with water, and rolls oily ink, which adheres exclusively to the greasy crayon marks. // door https://smarthistory.org/ // ref 9.2 atmospheric aerial perspective
