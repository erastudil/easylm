# The Stacks — Canonical Influential Thinkers Directory

Authoritative mapping of influential thinkers across the Dewey Decimal Classification System and EasyLM academic stacks. Each entry pairs the thinker's domain, key contribution, primary canonical text, their verified Wikipedia biographical portal, and the associated EasyLM textbook.

## 100 — Philosophy, Logic & Epistemology (`stacks/philosophy/`)

| Thinker | Era | Domain / Tradition | Core Contribution & Canonical Text | Wikipedia Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Socrates** | c. 470–399 BCE | Ancient Classical Greek | Dialectical inquiry (Socratic Method / *Elenchus*), ethics, care of the soul; transmitted in Plato's Early Dialogues (*Apology*, *Crito*). | [Socrates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socrates) |
| **René Descartes** | 1596–1650 | Continental Rationalism | Foundationalism, mind-body dualism, analytical geometry; *Meditations on First Philosophy* (*Cogito, ergo sum*), *Discourse on the Method*. | [René Descartes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Descartes) |
| **John Locke** | 1632–1704 | British Empiricism | Epistemology (*tabula rasa*), natural rights, social contract; *An Essay Concerning Human Understanding*, *Two Treatises of Government*. | [John Locke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Locke) |
| **Immanuel Kant** | 1724–1804 | German Idealism | Transcendental idealism, synthetic *a priori* knowledge, deontological ethics (Categorical Imperative); *Critique of Pure Reason*, *Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals*. | [Immanuel Kant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immanuel_Kant) |
| **Bertrand Russell** | 1872–1970 | Analytic Philosophy & Logic | Logicism, theory of definite descriptions, Russell's paradox, philosophy of mathematics; *Principia Mathematica* (with Whitehead), *The Problems of Philosophy*. | [Bertrand Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertrand_Russell) |
