# Comparative Religion & Mythology — Primary Academic Portals & Open Doors

1. **Internet Sacred Text Archive (ISTA)** — The largest freely available non-commercial digital archive of primary texts on religion, mythology, and folklore.
   https://www.sacred-texts.com/

2. **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Philosophy of Religion** — Scholarly articles on religious epistemology, the problem of evil, divine attributes, and comparative theology.
   https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/philosophy-religion/

3. **SuttaCentral** — Complete, multilingual open-access translations of early Buddhist discourses (Pali Nikayas and Chinese Agamas).
   https://suttacentral.net/

4. **Sefaria: A Living Library of Jewish Texts** — Open-access, interconnected digital corpus of Tanakh, Mishnah, Talmud, Midrash, and Kabbalah with English translations.
   https://www.sefaria.org/

5. **Quran.com** — Academic and multi-translation comparative corpus of the Holy Quran with morphological and linguistic analysis.
   https://quran.com/

6. **Joseph Campbell Foundation** — Educational archive preserving comparative mythology lectures, monomyth diagrams, and comparative symbol studies.
   https://www.jcf.org/

7. **Harvard Divinity School: Religious Literacy Project** — Academic profiles and pedagogical resources on living religious traditions.
   https://rpl.hds.harvard.edu/

8. **Perseus Digital Library: Classical Mythology** — Primary Greek and Roman mythological texts (Hesiod, Homer, Ovid) in Greek, Latin, and English translation.
   https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/

9. **Chinese Text Project: Daoist & Confucian Classics** — Annotated, cross-indexed classical Chinese philosophical and religious corpuses.
   https://ctext.org/

10. **The Pluralism Project at Harvard University** — Research mapping religious diversity, interfaith traditions, and constitutional freedoms.
    https://pluralism.org/
