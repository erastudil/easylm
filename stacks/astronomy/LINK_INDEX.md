# Astronomy & Astrophysics — Primary Academic Portals & Open Doors

1. **NASA Astrophysics Data System (ADS)** — Complete digital library portal for astrophysics papers, peer-reviewed literature, and preprints.
   https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/

2. **arXiv Astrophysics (astro-ph)** — Open access archive for scholarly articles in astrophysics, cosmology, and planetary science.
   https://arxiv.org/archive/astro-ph

3. **SIMBAD Astronomical Database** — CDS Strasbourg portal for cross-identifications, measurements, and bibliography for astronomical objects outside the solar system.
   https://simbad.u-strasbg.fr/simbad/

4. **NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory Solar System Dynamics** — Ephemerides, orbital parameters, and physical characteristics of planets and small bodies.
   https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/

5. **Ned Wright's Javascript Cosmology Calculator** — Flat, open, and cosmological parameter calculations using standard Friedmann models.
   https://www.astro.ucla.edu/~wright/CosmoCalc.html

6. **ESA Hubble & Webb Science Archive** — Open scientific datasets, spectra, and imaging from space-based astronomical observatories.
   https://archive.stsci.edu/

7. **OpenStax Astronomy 2e** — Peer-reviewed, open-access college textbook covering introductory to intermediate astrophysics.
   https://openstax.org/details/books/astronomy-2e

8. **European Southern Observatory (ESO) Science Archive** — Ground-based astronomical spectra, interferometry data, and survey catalogs.
   https://archive.eso.org/

9. **Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS)** — Public astronomical survey mapping galaxies, quasars, and spectroscopic stars.
   https://www.sdss.org/

10. **MIT OpenCourseWare Astrophysics** — Undergraduate lecture notes and problem sets in celestial mechanics and stellar astrophysics.
    https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/physics/
