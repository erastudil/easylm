# Psychology & Cognitive Science — Primary Academic Portals & Open Doors

1. **American Psychological Association (APA) Science Portal** — Guidelines, psychometric standards, and research syntheses across experimental and clinical psychology.
   https://www.apa.org/science

2. **National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH)** — Research on neurobiology, psychiatric classification, and evidence-based mental health clinical trials.
   https://www.nimh.nih.gov/

3. **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Cognitive Science** — Rigorous conceptual overviews of cognitive architecture, modularity, and philosophy of mind.
   https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/cognitive-science/

4. **Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences: Daniel Kahneman & Amos Tversky** — Foundational monographs and papers on Prospect Theory, heuristics, and judgment under uncertainty.
   https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2002/kahneman/facts/

5. **OpenStax Psychology 2e** — Peer-reviewed open-access college textbook covering biological bases, cognition, development, and social psychology.
   https://openstax.org/details/books/psychology-2e

6. **Psychological Review (APA Archives)** — Theoretical benchmarks in behavioral learning, signal detection theory, and memory models.
   https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/rev

7. **Society for Neuroscience (SfN)** — Primary research clearinghouse for neuroanatomy, neurotransmission, and cellular cognition.
   https://www.sfn.org/

8. **Cochrane Common Mental Disorders Group** — Systematic meta-analytic reviews of evidence-based psychological interventions and pharmacotherapies.
   https://cmd.cochrane.org/

9. **MIT OpenCourseWare Brain and Cognitive Sciences** — Undergraduate curriculum in neural substrates, psychophysics, and linguistic computation.
   https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/brain-and-cognitive-sciences/

10. **The Association for Psychological Science (APS)** — Open-access methodology benchmarks and replication standards in psychological science.
    https://www.psychologicalscience.org/
