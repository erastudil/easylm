## verification protocol

public-health claims → fetch the advisory itself (HHS / APA), not a news summary.
statutory claims → fetch the statute or regulator text (congress.gov, ftc.gov, ec.europa.eu).
survey numbers → fetch the methodologist's own methodology page (Pew publishes sample and dates).
empty hit → say unverified.
